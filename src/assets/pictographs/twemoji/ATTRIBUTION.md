This terminal bundles a curated subset of the original SVG files without
modifying the artwork. The sticker picker reuses selected artwork at a larger
display size. Files are bundled into the application; no remote image service
is needed for display, saved drawings, exports or printing.

The clean outline icons authored in `src/drawing-tools/pictographCatalog.ts`
are original application artwork and are not derived from Twemoji.
